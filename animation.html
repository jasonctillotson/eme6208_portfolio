Animation Project

Using Adobe, I took the following steps - all images were available in the Adobe stock image catalog. I first set a light, non-distracting background color to allow the vector images and text to stand out Then I added the title of the video at the top with a subtle drop-in effect Then I dropped the the images of the grapes, wine-presser and yeast using different animations for each, as well as a blur motion effect on the arrows to articulate the forward movement in time through the process. On the next scene, I added the clock image with a wiggle effect Then I added the wine bottle with a bob effect Then I added the glass with a jitter effect Finally, I added a scripted voice over to clearly articulate the process along with the images (I must say I had a hard time keeping the narration to <15seconds!, and then I uploaded the video to YouTube as an unlisted video. Hope this is educational for any wine enthusiasts out there!
